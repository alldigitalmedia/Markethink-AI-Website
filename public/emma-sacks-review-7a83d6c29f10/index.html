02 · What the audit tells us

Four openings Emma Sacks can take

Make the service clear

Replace the generic tagline with an audience, a service and a consultation CTA. Confirm how Miami and NY should be described.

Show the process

Use moodboards, material selection and render-to-real sequences. The audit cites the Plenitude Spa floor plan, render and final photograph as a useful format.

Put the founder in the story

Build trust through one founder-led reel each week, on camera or on site, rather than relying on the portfolio alone.

Build local relationships

Tag relevant vendors and clients; explore Miami showroom and furniture-brand collaborations; use specific local tags and reply to comments promptly.

Content directions for the next 30 to 60 days

The audit recommends a total cadence of 3 to 4 posts per week, including at least one founder-led reel. This is an editorial recommendation, not a new service commitment.

House rules for every post

Copy

Open with a hook, explain the design decision and end with a question or a relevant save, share or consultation prompt.

Visual system

Match graphic templates to the studio's typography and palette. Preserve the premium photography and avoid standalone tweet screenshots.

Reels

Show walkthroughs, before-and-afters, moodboards and material choices. Include the founder on camera or on site each week.

Community

Reply within the first hour. Tag vendors, brands and clients, ask them to share and engage with local architects, builders and real estate agents.

The creative principle

Show what it feels like to live there

Show the space. Explain the choices. Introduce the designer.

Make them imagine

Use a strong opening, then explain how a material, layout or texture changes the way a space feels.

Real projects, named

Use the studio's own project material, with accurate context. Keep renders distinct from finished-space photographs.

Minimal intervention

Let photography lead, with coherent graphic templates where they help explain a decision. Avoid screenshots that break the feed's aesthetic.

05 · Why this change

Give the portfolio a person, a story and a next step

The recommendation is to build on the visual identity, not replace it. A steady rhythm, useful explanations and a visible founder give prospective clients more reasons to remember the studio and start a conversation.

Consistency creates a rhythm

Address the long gaps recorded in the audit with a realistic 3 to 4 posts each week.

Founder presence builds familiarity

The niche references put a person behind the work. One weekly founder reel is the proposed starting point.

Process makes expertise visible

Show material choices, moodboards and render-to-real sequences rather than only finished spaces.

Education gives a reason to save

Marble choices, texture combinations and renovation-budget questions turn design knowledge into useful content.

Proof supports trust

Add real testimonials and before-and-afters when the studio supplies the supporting material.

Enquiries are the business measure

Review reach, saves, profile visits and link clicks monthly, then connect the work to enquiries from Instagram.

Audit observations

  • Irregular posting.
  • A generic bio without a CTA.
  • Limited founder visibility.
  • Few narrative reels or proof-led posts.

Recommended direction

  • 3 to 4 posts per week.
  • A clear service and consultation prompt.
  • At least one founder-led reel each week.
  • Process, education and genuine social proof.
