House rules for every post

Copy

The first line is a hook about the client's life, not about design. A question or a call to action at the end. English only.

Visual system

Full-bleed photography, text only on covers: Orpheus for the phrase, Caviar for the project name. Some posts are photos only.

Reels

Slow pans across real photographs, the phrase on the first frame and the project name on screen. Trending audio.

Community

Reply to every comment in the first hour. Collab posts with photographers, builders and furniture brands.

The creative principle

Show what it feels like to live there

The room is the backdrop. The life is the story.

Make them imagine

Sunday mornings in the kitchen, evenings on the terrace, a view from every window: phrases about life, not about finishes.

Real projects, named

Every piece shows a real, photographed project with its name, so admiration attaches to the studio, not to a generic image.

Minimal intervention

One phrase on the cover, the rest is photography. The work speaks; the words only open the door.

05 · Why this change

The feed should feel like walking into one of her homes

People hire an interior designer when they can picture their own life in the work. Today the feed shows rooms; the proposal is a feed that makes people imagine living there, and gives them one simple step to start.

Luxury is chosen with emotion

A phrase about Sunday mornings or evenings outside sells the experience before the finishes.

The benchmark proves it

Smaller studios multiply their views with a hook, on-screen text and a named project. None of that needs a face.

The photography is already there

Twelve projects shot to a professional standard: the plan starts now, with no new production.

A calm grid is a showroom

One visual system, minimal text and the brand's own fonts make the profile read as a premium studio in seconds.

Every piece leads somewhere

A consultation, the discovery questionnaire or a question in the comments: small, concrete steps instead of silence.

We measure what brings clients

Saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks and messages, not likes, so the plan adapts to what generates consultations.

Today

  • A gallery of finished rooms.
  • Vague captions cut at "more".
  • No call to action.
  • Reels with no hook.

Proposed

  • Named projects, told as a life.
  • One phrase on the cover, in the brand fonts.
  • A question or a consultation in every caption.
  • Two to three posts a week.
